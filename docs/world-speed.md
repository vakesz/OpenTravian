# World-speed profile

OpenTravian worlds may run at any installer speed from **1×** through **1_000_000_000×**. Economic speed scales construction, training, research, field production, and **merchant** capacity and travel (merchants always use the economic speed; at x10, troops move at 4× and merchants at 10×). Troop and hero map travel use a **moderated** `movement_speed_increase` from `Game\WorldSpeedProfile` (at 100×, troop travel is 20×; above 250× movement never drops when speed rises). Warehouse/granary capacity, cranny, trap and cage capacity stay **1× through 20×**, then scale on the high-speed curves; trap repair time is the exception and shortens from **11×** (a fixed minute up to 10×, then 60 s ÷ speed). Beginner protection follows the Legends table at 1×, 2×, 3×, 5× and 10× (5+3, 3+3, 3+3, 2+2 and 1+1 days). Speeds between those marks are interpolated. Above 10× both the basic window and the one extension shrink as 1 day × 10 / speed, and only then is each part held at 3 hours. Daily-quest cycle shortens above 20×. Hero health regeneration is a flat 10 % per day through x10, then 10·sqrt(speed ÷ 10) % per day, times the living-hero multiplier (1× through 20×, then (speed ÷ 20)^0.25). Hero revival, adventure spawn rate, festival duration and starvation follow smooth curves above the x1–x10 table (see `Game\WorldSpeedProfile`). Quest and reward tables use the speeds `{1,2,3,5,10,20}`. Adventure spawn stages use these 1× day bands: 3 per day on days 0-2, 2 on days 3-16, 1.5 on days 17-62, 1 from day 63. The stage lengths divide by the world speed and the per-day rates multiply by it, then by the high-speed spawn multiplier above 20×. They do not depend on the round length. Adventures never expire, at any speed: an open adventure waits until the hero has been on it, and its reward tier is the tier on offer on the day it appeared. Travel ETAs keep a whole-second floor. On speed worlds above 1×, Advanced setup can enable gold Finish now for queued Palace and Residence upgrades; Multihunter can change that world setting afterward. World Wonder construction is never eligible.

The values are derived in `game/include/Game/WorldSpeedProfile.php`; the installer (`Game\Installer\InstallInput::effectiveProfile`) applies any Advanced-mode overrides on top. Pick the speed at install time ([install.md](install.md)); change it later with `tools/set-world-speed.php` (see [operate.md](operate.md#change-the-world-speed)).

Culture point production (buildings and the hero's helmet, per real day) keeps the Legends values through 10× and is multiplied by speed ÷ 10 above that (100×: ×10, 10,000×: ×1000), so expansion keeps pace with the economy; the stored rates (`users.cp_prod`, `vdata.cp`) stay unscaled, and celebrations and one-off culture point rewards keep their own rules (`Game\WorldSpeedProfile::culturePointProductionMultiplier`, `tests/contracts/test-culture-point-speed-contract.php`). `tests/runtime/test-speed-matrix-runtime.php` pins construction, training, research, production, merchant, troop-speed, capacity, celebration and culture-point values at x1, x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20, x100 and x1000, and `tests/contracts/test-speed-matrix-contract.php` pins the profile bands. A new auction lasts floor(24 ÷ speed) hours and never under one hour: 24 h at x1, 12 h at x2, 8 h at x3, 4 h at x5 and 2 h at x10. Hero health regeneration is continuous and monotone.

## Hero item values and the artwork

Hero item effects do not scale with world speed (`heroConfig` rates are 1). The Helmet of the Consul gives +1600 culture points per day, scaled like building culture points (below), and the three horses are 14, 17 and 20 fields per hour at 1× (a step of 3). That mount speed is multiplied by the troop movement multiplier, and a mounted Gaul adds another 5 fields per hour times the same multiplier. Spurs add 3, 4 and 5 fields per hour at 1×, also times the movement multiplier. The artwork is the exception: its reward cap, share of production and cooldown are speed-banded in `Game\Hero\Artwork::rules()` (24 h up to 2×, 12 h at 3× and 5×, 6 h from 10×).

Ointments follow the same rule: each heals 1 % of hero health at every speed, up to 100 %. Used from the inventory they heal at once; equipped in the bag slot they heal a surviving hero right after a battle (`Game\Hero\BagOintment`), at most the health that battle took off. Level-up restores health to 100 % for a living hero (`Game\Hero\HeroExperience`). A hero that dies in the same action stays dead.

The auction house keeps a fee of every sale, at every speed: 2 % of the sale price, rounded up, taken from the seller's payout (`Game\Hero\AuctionFee`, `auction.feePercent` in the world configuration; 0 switches the fee and its confirmation dialog off). A sale price of 100 yields a fee of 2 and a payout of 98. The fee does not change with speed. `tests/runtime/test-auction-fee-runtime.php` pins the payout of a finished sale.

## Cancelling a construction

Cancelling a queued building or resource-field upgrade refunds the cost of the **previous level** (`Core\Village::cancelRefund`), and the full cost for an upgrade to level 1. The refund never exceeds what the order was charged, it does not depend on how far the order has progressed, and it follows the same cost branch (including the high-speed one) as the charge. The abort dialog shows the share as `floor(100 × previous wood cost ÷ new wood cost)`. Master Builder orders (charged when they start) refund nothing.

Examples of that previous-level refund: Cropland level 4 refunds 195/250/195/55 (60%); Granary level 8 refunds 350/440/310/90 (77%); Marketplace level 2 refunds 80/70/120/70 (80%). `tests/runtime/test-building-cancel-refund-runtime.php` checks these values at three progress points, and `tests/contracts/test-building-cancel-refund-contract.php` checks chained orders at x11.

## Extreme speeds (up to 1,000,000,000×)

The installer, `tools/set-world-speed.php` and the preflight accept 1× through 1,000,000,000× (`Game\Installer\InstallInput::MAX_SPEED`); the multiplier overrides share that ceiling. At every speed a construction level, a research and a troop movement take **at least one second**. Training and healing take at least **one tick of their queue clock**: one second up to 20×, one millisecond above 20× and one nanosecond above 20,000×, so on speed worlds a unit can train in under a second, and the engine credits every unit whose tick has passed in one pass (`tests/contracts/test-sub-second-training-contract.php`). World Wonder levels share that one second minimum. A celebration batch (cost and culture points scale with `ceil(speed ÷ 50)`) lasts one cool-down, which keeps shrinking with the speed (186 s at 1000×, 19 s at 10,000×, 1 s from 1,000,000×); festivals take at least 5 minutes, auctions 1 hour, Natar attacks on new villages 10 minutes. Trade ships sail deep water at 18 fields per hour times the economic speed, as merchants do on land; raiding and troop ships keep the movement band, and the hero's mount is 14, 17 and 20 fields per hour at 1× times the troop movement multiplier. Troop movement stops growing at 3000× the base speed (from about 37,500×). Columns that hold speed-scaled counts are wide: resources, production, capacity and troop counts are `DOUBLE`/`BIGINT`, farm-list troop counts and ship counts are `BIGINT UNSIGNED`, the archived speed of a finished round is `DECIMAL(13,2)`. HUD numbers above a million use the compact suffixes (`K`, `M`, `B`, `T`, `Q`, `Qi`, `S`). `tests/runtime/test-extreme-speed-runtime.php` pins all of this from x1000 to x1,000,000,000, and the speed-matrix contract carries those speeds on its monotone ladder.

## Rams and catapults

Rams and catapults use the normal Legends damage at every speed, including above 50×. `BattleSetter::fixSpeedCataRamEffect` returns that damage unchanged, and battles and the Rally Point simulator both call it.

## Speed-world shortcuts

The `speedShortcuts` world rule (off by default; suggested from 100×; set at install or on **World rules** in the control panel) adds conveniences for worlds where the ordinary queues are a chore. With the rule off, the shortcuts do nothing, except on a local development stack: where `TRAVIAN_DEV_TOOLS=1` (compose.dev.yaml sets it on the php service), they are always on, so a local world, already running or new, can be maxed out for gold without a reinstall.

- **Upgrade to max level** instantly sets a building to its max level for gold only (default 20 gold; `upgradeToMaxLevelGold` on **World rules**): no resources and no construction queue. The button is the yellow gold Gpack control. World Wonders never get it; Palace/Residence follow the gold Finish-construction world rule.
- **Train maximum** on every unit row of a troop building trains the whole affordable amount in one click (queued and paid like a typed order).
- **Smithy gold max** on the Smithy: each improvable unit row gets a gold **Upgrade to max level** button (default 20 gold) that sets that unit's smithy level to 20 immediately and clears its queued research; at the bottom, **Upgrade all to max level** (default 100 gold) does the same for every unit. Both charge gold only (no wood/clay/iron/crop). The prices are editable on **World rules** (`smithyMaxLevelGold`, `smithyUpgradeAllToMaxGold`).

`tests/runtime/test-max-level-upgrade-runtime.php`, `test-train-maximum-runtime.php` and `tests/contracts/test-smithy-gold-max-contract.php` pin the shortcuts.

**OpenTravian Plus and Gold club prices and duration.** Plus costs 10 gold. The Gold club costs 200 gold for the whole round. The prices are the world defaults `gold.plusGold = 10` and `gold.goldClubGold = 200` (`game/include/config.php`); they do not scale with speed. The Plus duration is speed-based (`Game\PremiumDuration::plusDefault`): 7 days at x1, 3 days at every speed from x2 through x10 (including speeds the quest tables do not list, such as x4), and above x10 three days shrink in proportion, 3 days × 10 / speed, never under one day: 36 h at x20, one day from x30 up, so **x100 is one day**. An admin override in hours (`admin.php?action=configurationDetails`) wins over the rule, but an override below 24 hours is saved as 24 hours and the admin help text says so. The resource-production bonus uses the same curve as Plus: 7 days at x1, 3 days from x2 through x10, then 3 days × 10 / speed and never under one day.

**Every bonus lasts at least one day.** OpenTravian Plus, the +25% lumber, clay, iron and crop production bonuses and every other timed bonus last at least 86,400 s, whether bought with gold, extended automatically or granted by a daily-quest or other reward. `Game\PremiumDuration::atLeastOneDay` is the one floor, applied where a bonus duration is computed: `PremiumDuration::plusDefault`, `configuredPlusDuration`, `officialDefault` and `configuredDuration` (the values `config.after.php` stores in `gold.plusAccountDurationSeconds` and `gold.productionBoostDurationSeconds`, which `GoldHelper` purchases and `AutoExtendModel` renewals read), the admin override save, `Model\Quest::calcEffect` (Plus and production-boost rewards) and the `DailyQuestModel` Plus and production-boost grants. `tests/contracts/test-premium-duration-contract.php` and `test-daily-quest-premium-duration-contract.php` pin the rule.
